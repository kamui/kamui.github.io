# Surface brief: jackchu.com homepage

Scope: the single-page homepage (layouts/index.html) plus the 404 page, which inherits it. Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience and job: people already interested in Jack (from a project, his resume, a job application or LinkedIn), then peers. Job: learn who he is and what he is up to now. An about-me page, not a hire-me page; no primary call to action. Email, GitHub, LinkedIn and the resume are quiet links.

Content: intro; About (story, hobbies, dated Now list); Experience (5 roles) with Education; Projects (Retriable 419M+ downloads, 900+ stars; Code Review Bench); Elsewhere (email, GitHub, LinkedIn, resume). No Twitter or Instagram. Photos optional; the selfie is kept as artist photo.

Anti-goals: slick or salesy; campy or cringey whimsy; vertical text nobody can read; fake Japanese copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Jack's site as a Tokyo boutique-pop CD single: a nearly bare cream sleeve with one mark, wrapped in a red paper obi crowded with his credits. Refuses the engineer-portfolio default of dark hero, name, and project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Inspiration palette, user-pinned: colors eyedropped from the rendered inspiration hero, not its swatch labels: obi red #C5062A (also the riso print color), sleeve cream #EFE4D1, warm white #FAF0E6 for type on the obi, ink black #111111, paper gray #DAD4CB. Red on cream is 4.8:1 and warm white on red is 5.4:1, so one red serves both. Six Caps ultra-condensed for the name and big titles, Barlow Condensed for labels and vertical credits, Barlow for reading. Vertical credit columns (one short English phrase each, read top to bottom), catalog number, STEREO/NYC box, barcode, numbered tracks, riso-duotone artist photo.

STORY: A visitor meets Jack's album: the cover says who he is, the liner notes tell his story and what is playing now, the tracklist is his work history, the singles are his projects, the closing obi credits say where to find him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail: stacked JACK CHU logotype and plain nav. Top right: a NOW line with a small waveform and a resume link. Center of the cream sleeve: one red guitar-pick mark. Lower 45%: full-width red obi with giant JACK CHU, three vertical intro phrases, catalog block, tracks 01-05 and two bonus tracks as vertical columns, credits, barcode.

FORM: Shibuya boutique sleeve (dealt challenger 1, user-chosen over the assigned Pedalboard); seed key ff7ad07e. Signature interaction: each obi band wraps on, sliding in from the side as it enters view, its credit columns printing in a quick stagger; reduced motion and no-JS show it in place. Motion grammar: band slide plus column stagger; the pick tilts on hover; the NOW waveform breathes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
